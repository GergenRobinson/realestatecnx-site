The basics

Work more than one market — maybe two states, two brokerages? Each market gets its own brokerage, ZIPs, and business lines, and the audit evaluates each separately.

License, MLS & public contact info

Licensed in more than one state, or in multiple MLSs? Add each one — every audit prompt will cover all of them.

More than one? Use commas to list multiple phones, emails, websites, or designations — for example, a different number per market. No need for a separate license entry just to add contact info. We use these to have the AI check NAP consistency — whether your Name, Address (brokerage), and Phone match everywhere online. Mismatches quietly hurt your Google ranking and AI visibility.
Your online profiles — paste the full link OR just your username/page name (we'll build the link). Commas for more than one on any field. Leave anything blank and the AI is instructed to hunt for it by name anyway.
Start here — it's the easy way. List every name, @handle, team brand, past name, or nickname you've ever posted or advertised under, and the AI hunts for all of it across every platform. Trouble? Quick fixes for common AI hiccups

It answered instantly with no sources. It skipped the web and answered from memory. Say: "Redo this using live web search, and cite the URL for every claim."

It says it can't browse or search. Web search is off or not on your plan. In ChatGPT, enable the Search option; in Claude, turn on web search in settings; or run the prompt in Perplexity, which always searches.

It stopped after a few areas. Normal for long audits — just say "continue". The prompt tells it to pick up exactly where it left off.

No progress line / no scorecard at the end. Say: "Follow the reporting format from my first message — progress footer while working, full scorecard table when done."

It couldn't open a specific site (some sites block AI). That's expected — the prompt tells it to say so and evaluate from search results instead. For a closer look, use the browser-mode add-on or try a different engine.

The scores feel too generous or too harsh. Say: "Re-check each score against the scoring rubric in my first message and show your reasoning." The rubric anchors are in the prompt precisely for this.

You need to leave mid-audit. Say "save code" and keep what it outputs — reopen the same chat later, or paste the code into a fresh chat to resume.
