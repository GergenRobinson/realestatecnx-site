📷 Tips for Best Results — Single Selfie

Use a well-lit, front-facing photo. Natural daylight near a window works best — avoid harsh overhead lighting or heavy shadows. Keep your face centered, unobstructed, and in sharp focus. Skip sunglasses, hats, or heavy filters. A neutral background is ideal, but the AI will replace it regardless.

For All Reference Photos: Higher resolution is always better. Minimum 512×512px, ideally 1024×1024 or larger. Make sure your face takes up at least 30–40% of the frame. Remove any watermarks. The AI will handle everything else — background, lighting, wardrobe, and composition — you just need a clear reference of you.
📷 Tips for Best Results — Existing Headshot

The higher the resolution, the better. A clean, evenly-lit photo gives the AI the most to work with. If your headshot has a busy background, that's fine — the AI will replace it. Avoid heavily retouched or stylized images, since the AI needs to read your real features. RAW or lightly edited photos produce the most accurate results.

For All Reference Photos: Higher resolution is always better. Minimum 512×512px, ideally 1024×1024 or larger. Make sure your face takes up at least 30–40% of the frame. Remove any watermarks. The AI will handle everything else — background, lighting, wardrobe, and composition — you just need a clear reference of you.
📷 Tips for Best Results — Multiple Photos

This gives the best likeness results. Include 3–5 images showing your face from slightly different angles — front, slight left, slight right. Mix lighting conditions if possible. Consistent photos from the same day work great, but variety helps the AI understand your features in 3D. Avoid group photos where your face is small or partially hidden.
